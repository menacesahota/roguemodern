FAQ

Common questions

How is this different from an agency?

Agencies sell retainers and decks. Rogue ships sites and systems for businesses that already make money offline - then operates alongside you. One stack: web, automation, AI.

Who do you work with?

Trade and local firms with demand and a dated web presence - packaging, print, joinery, fabrication, HVAC, electrical, and similar. If the business works and the site doesn't, we're interested.

What happens after I submit?

You get an email confirmation. We review the signal and reply with a next step - often a short teardown of what's dated and what to fix first.

Do I need a call first?

No. The teardown path is email-first. Calls happen when there's something worth building.
