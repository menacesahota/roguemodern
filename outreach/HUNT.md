# Rogue — client hunting

## Who to target
Solid local / trade businesses with money and a dated site:
- Packaging, printers, manufacturers
- Joiners, HVAC, electrical, roofing
- Accountants, solicitors, estate agents
- Wholesalers, food producers

Signals of "dated":
- Looks like 2008–2015
- Not mobile-friendly
- Flash / "Best viewed in…"
- Tiny text, cluttered homepage
- No clear "Get a quote" action
- Email is contactus@ or info@ on a GoDaddy parked-style page

## Daily hunt (45 mins)
1. Pick a town + trade (e.g. "joiners Watford")
2. Open Google Maps + first 2 pages of Google
3. Click every website — 10 seconds each
4. If dated → add to prospects.csv with one concrete clue
5. Find owner first name (About / Companies House / LinkedIn)
6. Find email (site contact page, or firstname@domain guess + verify later)
7. Send 5–10 first-touch emails max per day (protect domain reputation)

## Search starters
- `[trade] [town]`
- `[trade] [town] "get a quote"`
- site directories: Checkatrade, Yell — then open their actual website
- Competitors of people you already know

## Send rules
- Personalise with the dated clue
- Reply YES → send 3-point teardown
- Cap: ~10 new cold emails/day while warming `01@roguemodern.com`
- Track everything in prospects.csv

## Status values
new → researched → sent → replied → teardown_sent → call/booked → won / lost / nurture
